E. Model
V-JEPA 2 code: MIT, Copyright (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates (https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2/blob/main/LICENSE).
The app loads facebook/vjepa2-vitl-fpc16-256-ssv2 at revision 4aa02df8; its model card states licence: mit (https://huggingface.co/facebook/vjepa2-vitl-fpc16-256-ssv2). The app uses the 1024-d mean of last_hidden_state plus the checkpoint's own 174-d SSv2 classifier logits, joined into a 1198-d input for its own head trained on IBM clips (heads/frozen384.pkl).
Provenance of the checkpoint: it is a Meta model trained on Something-Something v2 (SSv2). The SSv2 data is separately licensed (Qualcomm "Data License Agreement - Research Use": https://www.qualcomm.com/content/dam/qcomm-martech/dm-assets/documents/jester_something_something_exercise_research_license_final_qti_28jul2022.pdf). This repository contains no Something-Something data and does not redistribute the weights; they download from Hugging Face on first run.
Open: whether the SSv2 data terms apply onward to use of this checkpoint is unresolved. No determination is made here and this is not licence advice. The research found no third-party checkpoint-use clause, and Meta labels the model card MIT. A separate research-only checkpoint (rep1106, h-only) is mentioned only if it ships; it does not ship here.
